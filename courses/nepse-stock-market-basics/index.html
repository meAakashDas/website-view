Comprehensive Curriculum

What You Will Learn

Designed specifically for the Nepal Stock Exchange. We teach concepts using real NEPSE charts, actual bank balance sheets, and practical frameworks.

NEPSE Market Fundamentals

Understand how the Nepal Stock Exchange operates, DEMAT & MeroShare setup, primary (IPO) vs secondary markets, SEBON rules, and broker systems.

Chart Reading & Price Action

Interpret Japanese candlesticks, identify primary trends, map horizontal support and resistance, and analyze price-volume relationships.

Technical Indicators & Setups

Master Moving Averages, Supertrend, MACD momentum analysis, and Fibonacci Retracements tailored specifically for NEPSE stock charts.

Fundamental Company Analysis

Evaluate financial statements, balance sheets, quarterly reports, EPS, P/E ratio, Book Value, and commercial bank performance metrics.

Systematic Stock Selection

Follow a proven evaluation framework to screen, analyze, and select sound investment opportunities in Nepal before committing capital.

Risk Management & Stop-Loss

Preserve capital with position sizing, risk-reward ratios, strict stop-loss rules, and emotional discipline to avoid common market traps.
