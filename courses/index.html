What You Will Learn

NEPSE Foundations & Investing Process

Understand how Nepal's stock market works, including SEBON, DEMAT, MeroShare, C-ASBA, and broker trading accounts.

Fundamental Analysis & Financial Reports

Learn to interpret company financial statements and evaluate EPS, P/E ratio, ROE, and book value.

Technical Analysis & Price Charts

Understand candlestick patterns, market trends, support and resistance, and price-volume relationships.
