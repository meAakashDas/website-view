Financial Jargon Pocket Reference Guide

Quick bilingual glossary of essential Nepal stock market (NEPSE), banking, tax, and investment terms.

Demat Account (BOID)NEPSE
डिम्याट खाता (१६ अंकको BOID)

An electronic account that holds your shares and bonds in digital, dematerialized form, similar to a bank account holding cash.

Nepal Rule: Issued by CDSC Depository Participants with annual maintenance fee of NPR 100.
MeroShareNEPSE
मेरोसेयर अनलाइन पोर्टल

The official CDSC web and mobile portal allowing retail investors to apply for IPOs via C-ASBA, check portfolio value, and execute EDIS share transfers.

Access: Available at meroshare.cdsc.com.np (Annual fee: NPR 50).
C-ASBA & CRNNEPSE
सी-आस्वा र CRN नम्बर

Centralized Applications Supported by Blocked Amount. A system that blocks your IPO application amount in your bank account until allotment.

Key Fact: Your bank provides a unique CRN (C-ASBA Registration Number) to authorize IPOs.
NEPSE TMSNEPSE
ट्रेड म्यानेजमेन्ट सिस्टम (TMS)

Trade Management System hosted by stockbrokers enabling retail investors to place live secondary market buy and sell orders on the NEPSE exchange.

Market Hours: 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM (Sunday through Thursday).
WACCNEPSE
औसत लागत (Weighted Average Cost of Capital)

The weighted average cost basis of your purchased shares. Used by MeroShare to accurately compute Capital Gains Tax upon selling.

Action: Mandatory to calculate in MeroShare 'My Purchase Source' before clearing EDIS.
EDISNEPSE
इलेक्ट्रोनिक डिपोजीटरी निर्देशन (EDIS)

Electronic Depository Instruction Slip. The digital process through which you authorize CDSC to transfer sold shares from your Demat to the buyer's broker.

Deadline: Must complete on MeroShare by T+2 settlement to avoid a severe 20% Close-Out Penalty!
LTP (Last Traded Price)NEPSE
अन्तिम कारोबार मूल्य

The most recent per-share execution price at which a transaction occurred during NEPSE trading hours.

Context: Portfolio market valuation is based on each stock's official daily closing LTP.
Circuit BreakerNEPSE
सर्किट ब्रेकर (कारोबार स्थगन)

Regulatory trading halts triggered when NEPSE index moves by 4% (20 min halt), 5% (40 min halt), or 6% (closure for the remainder of the day).

Individual Stocks: Capped at maximum daily price fluctuation band of +/- 10%.
Base RateBanking
बैंकको आधार दर (Base Rate)

The minimum benchmark lending interest rate below which commercial banks are legally barred from extending loans by Nepal Rastra Bank.

Loan Rate Formula: Your Effective Loan Interest Rate = Bank Base Rate + Fixed Premium Spread.
Interest Rate SpreadBanking
ब्याजदर अन्तर (Spread Rate)

The difference between the weighted average interest rate charged by a bank on loans and the interest rate paid on customer deposits.

NRB Cap: Strictly capped at a maximum ceiling of 4.00% for commercial banks.
CD RatioBanking
कर्जा-निक्षेप अनुपात (CD Ratio)

Credit-to-Deposit Ratio measuring total loans issued against total deposits and core capital. Reflects banking system liquidity.

Statutory Ceiling: Nepal Rastra Bank mandates a maximum ceiling of 90.00%.
Reducing Balance EMIBanking
घट्दो मौज्दात (Reducing Balance) EMI

Loan repayment structure where interest is calculated strictly on the remaining unpaid principal rather than the initial total borrowed amount.

Always Demand: Standard across Class-A banks; significantly cheaper than deceptive flat rates.
SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)Wealth
व्यवस्थित लगानी योजना (SIP)

An automated wealth-building mechanism of investing a fixed rupee amount (e.g. NPR 1,000/month) into open-ended mutual funds at regular intervals.

Advantage: Automates rupee-cost averaging via connectIPS without needing to time market tops or bottoms.
NAV (Net Asset Value)Wealth
खुद सम्पत्ति मूल्य (NAV)

The per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme, calculated by dividing total assets minus liabilities by outstanding units.

Par Value: Most schemes launch at NPR 10.00 base unit value during New Fund Offer (NFO).
Corporate DebentureWealth
ऋणपत्र (Debenture)

A long-term debt instrument issued by commercial banks carrying a guaranteed fixed annual coupon rate (e.g. 8.5% - 10.5%) paid semi-annually.

Safety: Backed by full bank balance sheet; safer than equity shares with predictable cash flow.
Capital Gains Tax (CGT)Tax
पुँजीगत लाभकर (CGT)

Statutory tax deducted at source on net profits realized from selling shares or real estate properties in Nepal.

NEPSE Rates: Individual Short-Term (<365 days): 7.5%; Individual Long-Term (>365 days): 5.0%.
Social Security Fund (SSF)Tax
सामाजिक सुरक्षा कोष (SSF)

State-administered pension and social protection fund with mandatory 31% monthly contribution (20% employer + 11% employee basic salary).

Tax Perk: SSF contributors are exempt from the 1% Social Security Tax on their base income slab!
Citizen Investment Trust (CIT)Tax
नागरिक लगानी कोष (NLK / CIT)

Government-backed retirement and savings institution providing tax-deductible voluntary pension schemes under Section 63 of Income Tax Act.

Tax Limit: Up to 1/3 of total income or NPR 3,00,000 max deduction per fiscal year.
SEBONTax
नेपाल धितोपत्र बोर्ड (SEBON)

Securities Board of Nepal. The apex statutory government regulator governing capital markets, stockbrokers, merchant bankers, and listed companies.

Role: Approves all IPO prospectuses, monitors insider trading, and sets broker commission slabs.
Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB)Banking
नेपाल राष्ट्र बैंक (केन्द्रीय बैंक)

The central monetary authority of Nepal responsible for currency issuance, monetary policy, banking supervision, and forex reserve management.

Consumer Protection: Regulates maximum loan fees, deposit insurance (NPR 5 Lakhs), and interest rate corridors.
