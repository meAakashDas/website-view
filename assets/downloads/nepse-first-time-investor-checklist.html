First-Time NEPSE Investor Checklist

Complete roadmap from opening your Demat account to trading your first stock on NEPSE TMS in Nepal.

Completion Progress 0 of 18 Steps Completed (0%)
Phase 1

Foundational Documents & KYC Prerequisites

  • Valid Citizenship Certificate (Nagarikta) or Passport
    Clear front and back copies with legible stamp and seal. Married women updating surname will need marriage registration certificate.
  • Individual Permanent Account Number (PAN)
    Obtain free in 15 minutes online via Inland Revenue Department (IRD) portal (ird.gov.np) or physical Taxpayer Service Office. Mandatory for all secondary share transactions exceeding NPR 5 Lakhs and C-ASBA linking.
  • Active Savings Account in a Commercial / Development Bank
    Account must be in your sole legal name with complete Biometric KYC updated. Online mobile banking activated for fast account verification.
  • Digital Passport Size Photographs & Residence Sketch Map
    Recent PP size photos with white background, digital signature sample on white paper, and ward/tole residence location route map.
Phase 2

Demat Account & MeroShare Portal Setup

  • Open Demat Account (16-Digit BOID) via Depository Participant (DP)
    Apply through your bank's capital subsidiary (e.g., Nabil Investment, Global IME Capital, Sanima Capital) or licensed stockbroker. Standard annual renewal fee: NPR 100.
  • Activate MeroShare Web & Mobile App Credentials
    Receive your DP Code, Username, and initial password from CDSC. Login to meroshare.cdsc.com.np and immediately update your master password.
  • Set 4-Digit MeroShare Transaction PIN
    Configure your dedicated 4-digit PIN under profile security. This PIN is mandatory for confirming IPO applications and EDIS share transfers.
  • Obtain C-ASBA Registration Number (CRN) from Bank
    Request CRN verification from your bank branch or online banking portal (Nabil, Sanima, NIC Asia allow instant self-generation under C-ASBA tab). Link it to your Demat.
Phase 3

Broker Selection & NEPSE TMS Trading Account

  • Select Registered Stock Broker (Broker 1 to 90+)
    Evaluate brokers based on payout settlement speed (T+2), customer service responsiveness, and local branch access.
  • Complete Online TMS Registration (tms[broker-no].nepalstock.com)
    Fill out NOTS online KYC with PAN, Bank Account Number, 16-digit BOID, citizenship scans, and photo signature. Receive your unique Client Code and TMS login link.
  • Link connectIPS Account for Instant Collateral Settlement
    Verify your mobile and bank on connectips.com. This enables 10-second trading collateral deposits on TMS and direct bank withdrawals upon selling shares.
Phase 4

Applying for Your First IPO (Initial Public Offering)

  • Check Active Public Issues on MeroShare
    Log in -> Click C-ASBA -> Click Apply for Issue. Review company prospectuses, credit rating (ICRA Nepal / CARE Nepal), and issue size.
  • Submit IPO Application (Standard 10 Kitta Rule)
    Select bank account, enter 10 Kitta (NPR 1,000 for standard par value NPR 100), enter your CRN Number, and authenticate with 4-digit PIN. Verify status switches to Verified.
  • Check Allotment Result on Official CDSC Portal
    Check allotment at iporesult.cdsc.com.np using your 16-digit BOID. If allotted, 10 Kitta will be credited to your Demat within 3–5 business days.
Phase 5

Secondary Market Trading & Post-Trade Settlement

  • Deposit Trading Collateral via connectIPS on TMS
    TMS Menu -> Fund Management -> Collateral Management -> Load Collateral. 1:4 trading limit allocated instantly for intraday/delivery trades.
  • Execute Limit Orders during NEPSE Trading Hours (11:00 AM - 3:00 PM)
    Trading runs Sunday through Thursday. Check Top 5 Bid/Ask Market Depth, specify Limit Price, and place buy/sell orders. Avoid market orders during high volatility.
  • Calculate Weighted Average Cost (WACC) & Holding Period in MeroShare
    When selling shares: MeroShare -> My Purchase Source -> Select scrip -> Calculate WACC -> Update Holding Period (determines Short-Term 7.5% vs Long-Term 5% Capital Gains Tax).
  • Transfer Sold Shares via EDIS before T+2 Deadline
    MeroShare -> My EDIS -> Transfer Shares -> Confirm with Transaction PIN. Failure to complete EDIS by 5:00 PM on T+1/T+2 results in 20% Close-Out Penalty under SEBON rules!
💡
Investor Discipline Rule: Never buy a stock solely because of social media hype, Viber/Telegram pump channels, or rumors. Review audited quarterly financial reports (Q1 to Q4), Earnings Per Share (EPS), Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio, and Reserve status before deploying hard-earned capital.
