Right Shares (Haqprada Share) in Nepal: Eligibility, Math & Action

Understand how right share offerings work in Nepal, how to apply at NPR 100 par via MeroShare, the mathematics of price adjustment, and the costly danger of forfeiting your rights.

01. What is this?

A Right Share is a capital-raising mechanism where a listed company gives its existing shareholders the statutory "pre-emptive right" to purchase newly issued shares at a heavily discounted face value of NPR 100 per unit before offering them to the external public. Common among hydropower companies repaying bank consortium debts or insurance companies meeting mandatory minimum capital thresholds set by the Nepal Insurance Authority (NPR 5 Arba for life insurers, NPR 2.5 Arba for non-life insurers), right shares require shareholders to actively commit new cash to claim their entitlement.

02. Why does it matter?

Unlike bonus shares (which are completely free), right shares cost you real money: NPR 100 per share. If a company announces a 1:1 right issue and you own 500 shares, you must deposit NPR 50,000 via MeroShare to exercise your rights. If you ignore the notice or lack the cash to apply, your existing shares will still be diluted by NEPSE’s price adjustment on book closure! Forfeiting your right shares without selling your holding prior to book closure is the fastest way to lose capital on NEPSE.

03. How does it work?

Step 01

Right Issue Ratio Declaration

The company announces its proposed right ratio (e.g., 1:0.65 or 65% rights) to raise capital for project expansion or regulatory compliance.

Step 02

SEBON Approval & Book Closure

After SEBON grants final approval, the company sets a book closure date. Only investors holding shares on the final trading day prior to this date qualify.

Step 03

Price Adjustment by NEPSE

On book closure morning, NEPSE resets the opening market price using the weighted average adjustment formula.

Step 04

Application Period via MeroShare

The issue opens for 21 to 35 days. Log in to MeroShare -> "Apply for Issue" -> "Right Share" -> Enter units and approve payment via C-ASBA.

Step 05

Auction of Unsold Units

Any units left unclaimed by existing shareholders are pooled and sold via competitive sealed-bid auction at a minimum bid of NPR 100.

The Nepal Context & Regulatory Reality

05. How does it apply in Nepal?

In Nepal’s hydropower sector, many promoters heavily leverage bank loans to construct run-of-river plants. Once commercial operation dates (COD) begin, companies frequently issue right shares to pay down high-interest bank consortium debts. Always investigate whether the right share capital is being used to retire expensive debt or being squandered on unproductive projects.

Regulatory Note: Statutory tax brackets, regulatory fees, and monetary caps reflect current official practice and are subject to periodic amendment in annual Nepal Finance Acts and NRB circulars. Always verify the latest gazetted directives before financial execution.

06. Practical Real-World Example in Nepal

Manish, 26, civil overseer in Hetauda

NPR 45,000 / month
Lost NPR 33,000 simply by ignoring a right share book closure notification
The Situation:

Manish owned 300 shares of a hydropower company trading at NPR 320 (portfolio value NPR 96,000). The company announced a 1:1 right share. Manish ignored the email and SMS alerts from CDSC because he was busy with site work and did not want to spend NPR 30,000.

The RisePaisa Strategy:

On the book closure date, NEPSE adjusted the stock price down to NPR 210: (320 + 100) / 2. Because Manish never applied for his 300 right shares, his 300 shares were now worth only 300 × NPR 210 = NPR 63,000. He suffered an immediate loss of NPR 33,000 without realizing it! Had he sold his shares before book closure or applied for the rights, his capital would have remained intact.

Sunil's 1:1 Right Share Dilemma: Applying with NPR 50,000 Cash vs Selling Before Book Closure

Goal & Dilemma: Protecting his capital in 500 shares of a hydropower company trading at NPR 350.

Option A: Hold through book closure but fail to apply for the right shares because cash is tight Catastrophic Permanent Loss

Price drops to NPR 225 on adjustment; his 500 shares drop from NPR 1,75,000 to NPR 1,12,500-losing NPR 62,500 permanently!

Option B: Sell all 500 shares at NPR 350 two days before book closure if cash is unavailable Capital Preserved & Recommended

Locks in the full unadjusted market price of NPR 1,75,000 in cash, completely avoiding the post-rights dilution drop.

Decision Takeaway: Never hold shares past book closure unless you have the cash ready to apply for your full right entitlement.

07. Advantages & Limitations

Key Advantages
  • Allows existing shareholders to acquire additional equity at par value (NPR 100), regardless of higher market price.
  • Helps capital-intensive companies (hydropower, insurance) retire expensive commercial bank debt and strengthen balance sheets.
  • Maintains your percentage ownership stake in the company, preventing dilution from outside parties.
  • Submitting right share applications via MeroShare C-ASBA is digital, secure, and fee-free.
Limitations & Risks
  • Requires real out-of-pocket cash commitment: NPR 100 per share must be funded from your bank account.
  • NEPSE mathematically adjusts the stock price downwards on book closure, penalizing investors who fail to apply.
  • If you do not apply, your eligible rights are forfeited without compensation and auctioned to third parties.
  • Issuing right shares increases total equity units, which dilutes EPS unless new projects boost profitability.

08. Who Should Use This (And Who Should Avoid It)

Ideal For:
  • Long-term shareholders with available cash who believe in the company’s management and future project cash flows.
  • Investors looking to average down their acquisition cost per share in fundamentally sound companies.
Caution / Avoid If:
  • Investors who lack cash to apply at NPR 100 per unit (must sell before book closure to avoid price adjustment losses).

09. Financial Formula & Step-by-Step Calculation

Mathematical Model

NEPSE Right Share Price Adjustment Formula

Padjusted = \frac{Pclose + (R × 100)}{1 + R}

Variable Breakdown:

Pclose
Last Traded Price (LTP)

Closing price of the stock on the last trading day before book closure.

R
Right Ratio

Ratio of right shares offered per existing share (e.g., 1 for 1:1, 0.5 for 1:0.5).

100
Par Issue Price

The statutory face value of NPR 100 per right share in Nepal.

Worked Nepal Scenario:

Stock price is NPR 500, issuing 1:1 right shares (R = 1): P_adjusted = (500 + 100) / (1 + 1) = 600 / 2 = NPR 300. If R = 0.5 (50% rights), P_adjusted = (500 + 50) / 1.5 = NPR 366.67.

10. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Nepal

Common Pitfall

Holding shares past book closure without having sufficient bank cash to apply for the right shares.

RisePaisa Best Practice

If you lack cash to apply at NPR 100, sell your shares BEFORE the book closure date to lock in the unadjusted market price.

Why: Holding past book closure without applying guarantees wealth destruction from downward price adjustment.
Common Pitfall

Assuming right shares are automatically credited like bonus shares.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Right shares require an active application and payment via MeroShare C-ASBA during the 30-day window.

Why: Unapplied shares are forfeited and auctioned off to third parties.
