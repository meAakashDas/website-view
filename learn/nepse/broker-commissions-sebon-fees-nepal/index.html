Broker Commissions, SEBON Fees & Taxes in Nepal: The Complete Math

Calculate the exact friction cost on every NEPSE trade - tiered broker rates (0.27%-0.40%), SEBON regulatory levy, DP fee, and Capital Gains Tax.

01. What is this?

Every transaction executed on the Nepal Stock Exchange entails regulatory, intermediary, and statutory costs. When you buy shares, you pay the purchase price plus broker commission, SEBON fees, and DP charges. When you sell shares, these transaction charges plus applicable Capital Gains Tax (CGT) are deducted from your gross proceeds before the net balance hits your bank account.

02. Why does it matter?

Frequent retail swing traders often celebrate small 2%-3% price gains without realizing that round-trip friction costs (buying fees + selling fees + DP fees + CGT) can consume 1.5% to 2.5% of the total trade. Understanding the exact fee schedule prevents you from overtrading and turning profitable moves into net losses.

03. How does it work?

Step 01

Identify Applicable Broker Commission Slab

SEBON mandates progressive broker commission: Up to NPR 50,000: 0.40% | NPR 50,001 to 5,00,000: 0.37% | NPR 5,00,001 to 20,00,000: 0.34% | NPR 20,00,001 to 1,00,00,000: 0.30% | Above NPR 1 Crore: 0.27%.

Step 02

Calculate Fixed Regulatory Charges

Multiply the transaction value by 0.015% (SEBON fee). Add flat NPR 25 for CDSC/DP settlement charges per company.

Step 03

Calculate Total Buying Cost

Buying Cost = Share Price × Quantity + Broker Commission + SEBON Fee + NPR 25 DP Fee. This total becomes your official WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital) in MeroShare.

Step 04

Calculate Selling Deductions & CGT

Selling Net = (Gross Sale Value) − Broker Commission − SEBON Fee − NPR 25 DP Fee − CGT. If you make a capital gain, CGT is 5% (if holding > 365 days) or 7.5% (if holding ≤ 365 days).

The Nepal Context & Regulatory Reality

05. How does it apply in Nepal?

In Nepal, Capital Gains Tax on secondary market equity trading is treated as a final withholding tax for individual retail investors. The Finance Act divides holding periods strictly at 365 days. If you sell a share on day 364, you pay 7.5% on net profits; if you wait until day 366, your tax drops to 5% - saving 33% in tax liability. For institutional investors, CGT is 10%.

Regulatory Note: Statutory tax brackets, regulatory fees, and monetary caps reflect current official practice and are subject to periodic amendment in annual Nepal Finance Acts and NRB circulars. Always verify the latest gazetted directives before financial execution.

06. Practical Real-World Example in Nepal

Binita, 34, business journalist in Kathmandu

NPR 60,000 / month salary
Friction fees and tax accounted for NPR 513.09 (17.1% of gross gain)
The Situation:

Binita bought 100 shares of a bank at NPR 300 (total NPR 30,000) and sold them 6 months later at NPR 330 (total NPR 33,000). She expected an exact NPR 3,000 profit.

The RisePaisa Strategy:

She calculated the full friction: Buy commission (0.40% = NPR 120), SEBON buy (NPR 4.50), DP buy (NPR 25) = Total buy cost NPR 30,149.50. Sale commission (0.40% = NPR 132), SEBON sell (NPR 4.95), DP sell (NPR 25). Gross profit = NPR 2,688.55. Short-term CGT (7.5%) = NPR 201.64. Net profit credited = NPR 2,486.91.

Madhav's Breakeven Calculation: Quick 2% Swing Trade vs Fee Reality

Goal & Dilemma: Selling NPR 100,000 worth of shares purchased yesterday after a minor 2% price increase.

Option A: Sell immediately assuming a net profit of NPR 2,000 Fee Illusion / Near Zero Net Profit

Buy & sell broker commission (~0.72%), SEBON fees, DP charges (NPR 50 total), and 7.5% short-term CGT consume nearly NPR 1,200, leaving barely NPR 800 profit for massive stress.

Option B: Hold quality shares across a 1-year+ horizon targeting 20%+ fundamental growth Tax & Cost Efficient

Reduces annual turnover friction, cuts CGT from 7.5% to 5.0%, and allows compounding to overcome small transaction fee percentages.

Decision Takeaway: Every time you trade on NEPSE, the broker, SEBON, and the government get paid guaranteed fees; only you take the market risk. Trade less, hold longer.

07. Advantages & Limitations

Key Advantages
  • Understanding exact transaction fee tiers prevents hidden frictional costs from eroding short-term profits.
  • SEBON's regulated progressive commission slabs (0.24% to 0.36%) protect retail investors from broker overcharging.
  • Clear accounting of DP fees (NPR 25 per transaction), SEBON fee (0.015%), and capital gains tax (CGT).
  • Encourages longer holding periods and less frequent churning to minimize deadweight fee loss.
Limitations & Risks
  • High-frequency day trading incurs massive cumulative fee drag on capital.
  • Even on losing trades, broker commission, SEBON regulatory fee, and DP fees must still be paid.
  • Short-term capital gains tax is 7.5% (under 365 days) compared to 5% for long-term investors (over 365 days).
  • Requires tracking your adjusted Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) across multiple purchases.

08. Who Should Use This (And Who Should Avoid It)

Ideal For:
  • Active secondary market equity and debenture investors in Nepal.
  • Traders evaluating their true breakeven prices before entering sell orders.
  • Anyone calculating net post-tax returns on their NEPSE portfolio.
Caution / Avoid If:
  • Exclusive IPO participants (who pay zero broker commissions on primary issuances).

09. Financial Formula & Step-by-Step Calculation

Mathematical Model

Complete Round-Trip Trading Cost Formula

Net Profit = (Sgross - Bgross) - (Cbuy + Csell) - (Fsebon × 2) - 50 - CGT

Variable Breakdown:

Sgross
Gross Sale Proceeds

Quantity of shares sold multiplied by selling price.

Bgross
Gross Purchase Cost

Quantity of shares bought multiplied by purchase price.

Cbuy / Csell
Broker Commissions

Tiered commission (0.27% to 0.40%) on purchase and sale transactions.

50
Combined DP Charges

NPR 25 charged on buy + NPR 25 charged on sell.

CGT
Capital Gains Tax

5% (holding > 365 days) or 7.5% (holding <= 365 days) applied only on net capital gain.

Worked Nepal Scenario:

Buy NPR 1,00,000, sell at NPR 1,20,000 after 400 days. Broker fees = 0.37% * 100K + 0.37% * 120K = NPR 370 + 444 = NPR 814. SEBON fees = 0.015% * 220K = NPR 33. DP fees = NPR 50. Net gain before tax = NPR 19,103. Long-term CGT (5%) = NPR 955.15. Final in bank = NPR 18,147.85.

10. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Nepal

Common Pitfall

Selling shares on Day 360 instead of waiting for Day 366.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Holding for 366 days reduces your Capital Gains Tax from 7.5% to 5.0%.

Why: Waiting just 6 more days saves 33% of your tax burden under Nepal's 365-day holding threshold.
Common Pitfall

Buying micro-lots of 10 shares across multiple transactions.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Consolidate purchases into larger lots to minimize the impact of the flat NPR 25 DP fee.

Why: Paying NPR 25 DP fee on a NPR 1,500 micro-trade adds an immediate 1.67% deadweight loss.
Common Pitfall

Forgetting that CGT is only charged on profitable trades.

RisePaisa Best Practice

If you sell at a loss, your Capital Gains Tax is NPR 0; only broker, SEBON, and DP fees apply.

Why: Taxes only apply to positive net capital appreciation after deducting statutory purchase costs.
