How Much Life Insurance (Sum Assured) Do You Really Need in Nepal?

Calculate your exact Human Life Value (HLV) - why an expensive NPR 5 Lakh endowment policy leaves your family broke, and how to secure NPR 1 Crore cover.

01. What is this?

Sum Assured (Bimāṅka Rakam) is the guaranteed financial payout that your life insurance company pays to your nominated beneficiaries if you pass away during the policy term. Calculating your required coverage is a mathematical assessment based on Human Life Value (HLV) that ensures your family can clear debts, maintain their current lifestyle, and fund children's higher education without the primary earner's salary.

02. Why does it matter?

Insurance agents in Nepal earn 15% to 25% commissions selling traditional endowment (Sāwadhik) policies. Consequently, they convince families to buy NPR 5 Lakh policies with hefty NPR 40,000 annual premiums. If the breadwinner dies, an NPR 5 Lakh payout will be completely exhausted within 12 to 18 months in Kathmandu or Pokhara. For that same NPR 40,000 annual premium, a pure Term Life policy secures NPR 1 Crore to 1.5 Crore in coverage, protecting the family for two decades.

03. How does it work?

Step 01

Calculate Annual Household Expenses

Isolate what your family strictly needs to survive each year without your personal discretionary expenses: rent/maintenance, groceries, children's schooling, utilities, and healthcare (e.g., NPR 5,00,000/year).

Step 02

Apply the 15x Income Multiplier

Multiply annual living expenses by 15. At a 7% bank fixed deposit yield, an invested corpus of 15x expenses generates the required annual family living cash flow indefinitely without touching the principal.

Step 03

Add All Liabilities & Major Future Goals

Add total outstanding debts (home loan, auto loan, personal debt) plus future non-negotiable milestones (e.g., college tuition NPR 15 Lakh per child).

Step 04

Subtract Existing Liquid Assets

Deduct existing productive assets (bank savings, mutual funds, shares, existing policies). The resulting balance is your true required Sum Assured.

The Nepal Context & Regulatory Reality

05. How does it apply in Nepal?

Under regulations from the Nepal Insurance Authority (Nepal Beema Pradhikaran), licensed life insurers (such as Nepal Life, National Life, LIC Nepal, and Himalayan Life) offer term life plans. However, retail term adoption remains low because agents actively conceal term plans due to minimal commissions. Savvy consumers specifically ask for "Myadi Jeevan Beema" (Pure Term Life) to buy adequate multi-crore protection affordably.

Regulatory Note: Statutory tax brackets, regulatory fees, and monetary caps reflect current official practice and are subject to periodic amendment in annual Nepal Finance Acts and NRB circulars. Always verify the latest gazetted directives before financial execution.

06. Practical Real-World Example in Nepal

Sunil, 33, private company branch manager in Butwal

NPR 70,000 / month salary (Family expenses NPR 45,000/month)
Increased life cover from NPR 8 Lakh to NPR 1 Crore while saving NPR 24,000/year
The Situation:

Sunil was paying NPR 52,000/year for an endowment policy with a sum assured of NPR 8 Lakh. He had a home loan of NPR 35 Lakh and two young children aged 3 and 6.

The RisePaisa Strategy:

He calculated his real vulnerability: if he passed away, his NPR 8 Lakh insurance wouldn't even pay a quarter of his NPR 35 Lakh home loan, leaving his family bankrupt. He bought a 25-year pure Term Life policy with a Sum Assured of NPR 1 Crore for an annual premium of NPR 28,000. He used the remaining NPR 24,000 to start an open-ended mutual fund SIP.

Goal & Dilemma: Determining proper life insurance cover on an annual salary of NPR 800,000 with a home loan of NPR 30,00,000.

Option A: Purchase a standard NPR 5,00,000 policy recommended by an insurance agent relative Catastrophically Underinsured

NPR 5 Lakh does not even cover 20% of his home loan, leaving his grieving family facing immediate bank foreclosure.

Option B: Calculate Cover using DIME method: NPR 30L Debt + NPR 80L (10x income) = NPR 1.10 Crore Term Cover Scientifically Sound & Recommended

If Binod passes away, the payout clears the NPR 30 Lakh mortgage immediately; the remaining NPR 80 Lakh in an 8% bank FD generates NPR 6.4 Lakh annual income for his family for life.

Decision Takeaway: A life insurance policy that pays your family less than 10 times your annual income is not a safety net; it is just a token. Calculate your true Human Life Value.

07. Advantages & Limitations

Key Advantages
  • Applies the scientific Human Life Value (HLV) and DIME methods (Debt + Income Replacement + Mortgage + Education).
  • Prevents dangerous underinsurance where families receive nominal sums that deplete within 1-2 years.
  • Calculates the exact replacement capital needed to generate risk-free interest covering family living costs.
  • Factor in existing debt liabilities so dependents aren't forced into asset foreclosure.
Limitations & Risks
  • Inflation increases future educational and living costs, requiring life cover reassessments every 5 years.
  • Medical underwriting requirements become stricter for sum assured covers exceeding NPR 50 Lakhs to 1 Crore.
  • Does not replace the need for health insurance (life insurance pays out only upon death or permanent total disability).
  • Overestimating cover can lead to unnecessary premium drag if family financial independence is already achieved.

08. Who Should Use This (And Who Should Avoid It)

Ideal For:
  • Primary family breadwinners determining how much insurance cover to buy.
  • Borrowers taking new home or business loans exceeding NPR 20 Lakhs.
  • Parents calculating long-term higher education funding requirements for their children.
Caution / Avoid If:
  • Children, dependents, or non-earning family members (who have zero economic human life value).

09. Financial Formula & Step-by-Step Calculation

Mathematical Model

Human Life Value (HLV) Needs Method Formula

Sum Assured = (Eannual × 15) + Total Liabilities + Future Milestones - Liquid Assets

Variable Breakdown:

Eannual
Annual Family Living Expense

Annual cost required to maintain your family's standard of living.

15
Capitalization Multiplier

Multiplier ensuring a 7% bank deposit yield produces replacement income.

Total Liabilities
Outstanding Debt

Total home loan, auto loan, and personal bank liabilities.

Liquid Assets
Existing Productive Capital

Current savings, FDs, mutual funds, and shares available immediately.

Worked Nepal Scenario:

Annual family expenses = NPR 6,00,000. 15x multiplier = NPR 90,00,000. Home loan balance = NPR 30,00,000. Children higher education fund = NPR 20,00,000. Existing savings = NPR 15,00,000. Required Sum Assured = 90,00,000 + 30,00,000 + 20,00,000 - 15,00,000 = NPR 1,25,00,000 (1.25 Crore).

10. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Nepal

Common Pitfall

Buying life insurance for children who earn zero income.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Only insure income earners whose death causes catastrophic financial loss to dependents.

Why: Insurance replaces economic earning power; children have no income to replace.
Common Pitfall

Treating life insurance primarily as a savings or investment product.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Separate insurance from investment: buy pure term life for protection, invest in SIPs for wealth.

Why: Endowment policies deliver poor 4%-5% annual returns that fail to beat Nepal's 7% inflation.
Common Pitfall

Failing to increase coverage when taking a massive new home loan.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Ensure your sum assured covers your total mortgage balance so your family doesn't lose the home.

Why: Banks will foreclose on the house if the breadwinner dies and life insurance cannot clear the mortgage.
