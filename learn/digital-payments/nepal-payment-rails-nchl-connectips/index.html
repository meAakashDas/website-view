Nepal's Digital Payment Backbone: How NCHL, connectIPS & RTGS Move Money

Understand the underlying plumbing of Nepal's digital economy - from real-time interbank rails to bulk government settlements.

01. What is this?

Nepal's payment rails are the secure digital highways that move funds between different banks, payment service providers (wallets), and government agencies. Operated primarily by Nepal Clearing House Limited (NCHL) under Nepal Rastra Bank regulation, these rails include connectIPS for instant retail transfers, RTGS for massive institutional settlements, and NCHL-IPS for bulk batch payments.

02. Why does it matter?

Ten years ago, moving money from a bank in Kathmandu to an account in Pokhara required carrying paper cheques, physical clearing visits, and days of waiting. Today, understanding which payment rail to use saves you time and fees. Using connectIPS for an NPR 5 Lakh transfer costs just NPR 8 and settles in 3 seconds, whereas an improper counter transfer might cost NPR 200 and take 24 hours.

03. How does it work?

Step 01

Retail Transaction Initiated via connectIPS

When you transfer funds on connectIPS, the system performs real-time account validation and locks the money instantly in the sender's bank account.

Step 02

Real-Time Clearing & Multilateral Settlement

The transfer instruction is routed through NCHL's central switch, crediting the beneficiary account instantly. Net interbank settlement occurs through banks' reserve accounts at NRB.

Step 03

Bulk Batch Clearing via NCHL-IPS

For enterprise payrolls and stock dividends, companies upload bulk lists to CorporatePAY. NCHL-IPS processes these in structured clearing sessions throughout the day.

Step 04

High-Value Settlement via RTGS

Transactions exceeding NPR 20 Lakh bypass retail switches and settle gross, transaction-by-transaction, directly on Nepal Rastra Bank's core banking books in real time.

The Nepal Context & Regulatory Reality

05. How does it apply in Nepal?

Prior to NCHL's establishment in 2008, all cheque settlements in Nepal were performed manually by bankers physically meeting at the NRB clearing house in Thapathali. Today, NCHL processes millions of digital transactions monthly. The National Payment Interface (NPI) has unified government revenue collection: citizens now pay inland revenue taxes, vehicle renewal fees, and passport fees online with automated revenue code reconciliation directly into the government treasury.

Regulatory Note: Statutory tax brackets, regulatory fees, and monetary caps reflect current official practice and are subject to periodic amendment in annual Nepal Finance Acts and NRB circulars. Always verify the latest gazetted directives before financial execution.

06. Practical Real-World Example in Nepal

Sujan, 32, e-commerce store owner in Kathmandu

NPR 1,80,000 / month gross revenue
Settled an NPR 4.5 Lakh interbank supplier payment on a weekend in 4 seconds
The Situation:

Sujan needed to pay a garment supplier in Birgunj NPR 4,50,000 urgently on a Saturday afternoon to release festive Dashain stock. His bank branch was closed until Sunday.

The RisePaisa Strategy:

Sujan logged into his connectIPS web portal, verified his token, and transferred the NPR 4,50,000 directly to the supplier's account at a different commercial bank. The transaction settled in 4 seconds for a fee of just NPR 8. The supplier verified receipt and dispatched the cargo that very evening.

Manoj's Tax Payment: Paying NPR 80,000 via Wallet vs ConnectIPS Government Tax Portal

Goal & Dilemma: Paying annual business income tax of NPR 80,000 to the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

Option A: Try paying through a digital wallet with a single transaction limit of NPR 25,000 Limit Block & High Fees

Requires multiple wallet top-ups, incurs wallet loading charges, hits daily wallet outflow limits, and risks transaction timeouts.

Option B: Pay directly through the IRD Taxpayer Portal using ConnectIPS with the official EBP Number Direct, Instant & Recommended

Transfers the entire NPR 80,000 directly from his bank account to the Government Treasury account for a flat NPR 8 fee, delivering an instant verified IRD tax clearance voucher.

Decision Takeaway: ConnectIPS is Nepal's sovereign national payment backbone. Use it for high-value transfers, tax clearance, and capital market investments.

07. Advantages & Limitations

Key Advantages
  • Direct account-to-account (A2A) interbank settlement regulated by NRB through Nepal Clearing House Ltd (NCHL).
  • Empowers heavy-duty transactions: pay government taxes, vehicle revenue, customs duty, and capital market IPO/SIP payments.
  • Vastly higher daily and monthly transaction limits compared to consumer digital wallets (eSewa/Khalti).
  • Eliminates wallet intermediary custody risk: funds transfer directly between sovereign commercial bank accounts.
Limitations & Risks
  • Initial registration requires linking your bank account and completing electronic or branch mandate verification.
  • Transaction fees (NPR 2 to 8 per transfer) apply, whereas merchant QR payments on Fonepay are free for buyers.
  • Requires active internet connectivity and two-factor OTP/transaction password authentication.
  • Interbank return or reversal of erroneous account numbers requires formal NCHL clearing dispute resolution.

08. Who Should Use This (And Who Should Avoid It)

Ideal For:
  • Business owners, traders, and accountants paying government taxes and corporate vendors.
  • Secondary stock market investors funding broker TMS collateral and mutual fund SIPs.
  • Anyone transferring funds exceeding NPR 25,000 between different Nepali commercial banks.
Caution / Avoid If:
  • Retail shoppers paying for small micro-groceries or street food (better suited for merchant QR codes).

10. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Nepal

Common Pitfall

Using mobile banking interbank transfer for large amounts without checking higher fees.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Use connectIPS web for transfers up to NPR 20 Lakh to benefit from the flat NPR 8 fee cap.

Why: Some mobile banking interbank transfers charge NPR 10-11 per transaction with lower per-transaction caps.
Common Pitfall

Attempting to send RTGS payments outside central bank clearing hours.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Initiate RTGS transactions during banking working hours (typically 10 AM to 3 PM on weekdays).

Why: Unlike connectIPS which operates 24/7/365, RTGS requires the NRB central settlement window to be open.
Common Pitfall

Typing the wrong account number or branch name on connectIPS.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Double-check account number carefully; connectIPS matches name and account number against the destination core banking system before confirming.

Why: Inputting an incorrect account number requires formal interbank dispute resolution that can take days to reverse.
