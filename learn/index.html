Finance Glossary Preview

Demystifying essential financial jargon used in Nepal.

View Complete Glossary
A

Asset (सम्पत्ति)

A resource with economic value that generates passive cash flow or appreciates over time (e.g. shares, real estate).

B

Budget (बजेट)

A structured plan allocating your net monthly earnings across necessities, lifestyle, and high-yield savings.

C

Capital Gain (पुँजीगत लाभ)

The profit earned from selling an investment (e.g. NEPSE shares or land) higher than its purchase cost.

D

Dividend (लाभांश)

A portion of net company profits distributed to shareholders in cash or additional bonus shares in Nepal.

E

ETF (एक्सचेन्ज ट्रेडेड फण्ड)

An investment fund traded on the stock exchange that holds a basket of underlying securities matching an index.

F

Fixed Deposit / FD (मुद्दती निक्षेप)

A high-interest bank deposit locked for a set tenure (e.g. 1-5 years) yielding guaranteed periodic interest.

G

GDP (कुल गार्हस्थ्य उत्पादन)

Gross Domestic Product: the monetary measure of all final goods and services produced within Nepal annually.

H

HLV (Human Life Value)

The economic value of an individual’s future earning capacity used to determine adequate Term Life cover.

I

IPO (Initial Public Offering)

The first time a private company sells newly issued shares to the public in Nepal at face value (NPR 100).

K

KYC (Know Your Customer)

The mandatory regulatory verification of an account holder’s identity and physical address by banks in Nepal.

M

MeroShare (मेरोसेयर)

The official digital web platform provided by CDSC for applying for IPOs, reviewing Demat, and EDIS transfer.

N

NAV (Net Asset Value)

The per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme calculated by dividing net assets by total units.

P

PAN (स्थायी लेखा नम्बर)

Permanent Account Number issued by the Inland Revenue Department for tracking tax compliance and TDS.

Q

QR Payment (Fonepay / NepalPay)

Standardized EMVCo QR code protocol enabling instant direct bank-to-bank retail payments across Nepal.

R

Repo Rate (रिपो दर)

The policy interest rate at which Nepal Rastra Bank lends short-term liquidity to commercial banks.

S

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

An investment approach where a fixed rupee sum is deposited into open-ended mutual funds every single month.

T

TDS (कर कट्टी / स्रोतमा कर)

Tax Deducted at Source: statutory advance tax withheld on salary, bank interest, or freelance payments in Nepal.

V

VAT (मूल्य अभिवृद्धि कर)

Value Added Tax: a standard 13% indirect consumption tax levied on goods and services in Nepal.
