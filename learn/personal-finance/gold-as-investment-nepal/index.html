Gold as an Investment in Nepal: Ornament vs. Wealth Preservation

Understand how gold acts as a hedge against currency depreciation in Nepal, why jewelry making charges (jhyal/jarti) destroy investment returns, and how to buy bullion coins safely.

01. What is this?

Gold is a precious metal that has served as a universal store of value for thousands of years. In Nepal, the domestic price of gold is set daily by the Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association (FENEGOSIDA / नेपाल सुनचाँदी व्यवसायी महासंघ), calculated directly from international bullion prices in US Dollars, the NPR/USD exchange rate, and government customs import duties (भन्सार महसुल). While gold is deeply intertwined with Nepali culture, weddings, and festivals (Teej, Dashain, Dhanteras), there is a critical distinction between purchasing gold jewelry for personal wear and investing in physical 24-karat gold bullion for wealth preservation.

02. Why does it matter?

Most Nepali families believe that buying gold jewelry is an investment. However, when you sell jewelry back to a jeweler, they deduct making charges (ज्याला), wastage loss (जर्ती - often 5% to 10%), and stone weight, causing an immediate 15% to 25% haircut on your capital. To break even on gold jewelry, domestic gold prices must rise by at least 20%! Furthermore, holding physical gold in your home carries burglary risk, requiring bank locker rental expenses. Understanding the math behind gold prevents costly purchasing mistakes.

03. How does it work?

Step 01

Understand Daily FENEGOSIDA Rates

Track official bullion rates set every morning at 10:30 AM for Fine Gold (24 Karat - छापावाल सुन) and Tejabi Gold (22 Karat - तेजाबी सुन) per Tola (11.664 grams).

Step 02

Separate Ornament from Investment

If buying for weddings, accept that making charges and gemstone costs are lifestyle expenses. If buying for wealth storage, buy pure 24-karat gold coins (असर्फी) or mint bars.

Step 03

Verify Hallmark Purity & Bill of Sale

Ensure your gold coin or bar is certified with an official 999 hallmark stamp and obtain a VAT/PAN tax invoice stating the exact weight in grams to guarantee future buyback value.

Step 04

Secure Bank Locker Storage

Never store substantial gold bullion at home. Rent a secure Class A bank locker (annual fee NPR 2,500 to NPR 5,000) to eliminate burglary and insurance risks.

The Nepal Context & Regulatory Reality

05. How does it apply in Nepal?

In Nepal, commercial banks are the sole authorized importers of gold bullion, regulated by Nepal Rastra Bank with daily import quotas (typically 10kg to 20kg per day). During festival spikes (Dhanteras and wedding seasons), artificial shortages often lead local jewelers to charge unauthorized premiums. Buying Nepal Rastra Bank Mint Division (टक्सार महाशाखा) minted gold Asarfis provides guaranteed purity at government-fixed rates.

Regulatory Note: Statutory tax brackets, regulatory fees, and monetary caps reflect current official practice and are subject to periodic amendment in annual Nepal Finance Acts and NRB circulars. Always verify the latest gazetted directives before financial execution.

06. Practical Real-World Example in Nepal

Bikram and Sunita, 32, newlyweds in Pokhara

Combined salary: NPR 90,000 / month
Saved NPR 37,000 in upfront making/wastage losses by choosing bullion coins over jewelry
The Situation:

Sunita received NPR 2,00,000 as wedding cash gifts and wanted to "invest it in gold". She visited a local jeweler and was persuaded to buy an ornamental gold necklace with intricate stone work weighing 1.5 Tolas for NPR 2,10,000 (including NPR 22,000 in making charges and stone charges).

The RisePaisa Strategy:

Had they needed emergency cash 6 months later, the jeweler would have deducted the NPR 22,000 making fee plus 8% jarti (NPR 15,000), returning only NPR 1,73,000 (a loss of NPR 37,000!). Learning this before purchase, Bikram advised buying two 1-Tola 24K gold Asarfi coins with an official hallmarked receipt for NPR 2,05,000. Five years later, with gold prices rising 35%, their coins appreciated cleanly to NPR 2,76,000 with zero deduction upon resale.

Goal & Dilemma: Investing NPR 2,00,000 for emergency family wealth preservation over 5 years.

Option A: Purchase an ornamental 22K gold necklace with stones and intricate craftsmanship High Loss on Day 1

Loses NPR 25,000 to NPR 35,000 immediately in making charges and stone deductions upon resale.

Incurs minimal minting spread; 100% of the gold weight is preserved and converted at full bullion rates.

Decision Takeaway: Jewelry is a lifestyle expense. If your objective is wealth preservation, buy pure 24K bullion Asarfi coins only.

07. Advantages & Limitations

Key Advantages
  • Provides an unassailable sovereign hedge against the long-term depreciation of the Nepali Rupee.
  • Universally liquid asset in Nepal: convertible to immediate cash within 15 minutes at any jeweler.
  • Zero counterparty risk when held physically in pure 24K coin/bullion form (cannot go bankrupt like a company).
  • Deep cultural acceptance and emergency collateral utility for bank gold loans (सुन कर्जा).
Limitations & Risks
  • Produces zero ongoing cash flow: no dividend, no interest, and no rental yield.
  • Jewelry incurs heavy making charge (Jhyal) and melting wastage (Jarti) haircuts of 15% to 25% on resale.
  • Physical holding introduces burglary risk, necessitating annual bank locker rental expenses.
  • Long-term returns trail behind compounding equity index returns in NEPSE over 20-year periods.

08. Who Should Use This (And Who Should Avoid It)

Ideal For:
  • Investors seeking a conservative crisis hedge against hyperinflation and currency depreciation.
  • Families allocating 5% to 10% of their net worth to tangible emergency stores of value.
  • Individuals buying 24K Asarfi mint coins for long-term generational preservation.
Caution / Avoid If:
  • Young investors in their 20s and 30s seeking rapid compound capital growth (better suited for SIPs and equities).
  • Anyone intending to treat stone-studded ornamental jewelry as a liquid investment.

09. Financial Formula & Step-by-Step Calculation

Mathematical Model

Gold Resale Realization Formula in Nepal

Net Resale Value = (Weight in Tolas × (1 - J)) × Pbuyback - D

Variable Breakdown:

J
Jarti (Wastage) Percentage

Typically 0% for 24k bullion coins, but 5% to 10% for ornamental jewelry.

Pbuyback
Current FENEGOSIDA Rate

Current daily buying rate per Tola published by the association.

D
Stone & Making Deductions

Non-gold weights deducted during jeweler assessment.

Worked Nepal Scenario:

Jewelry weighing 1 Tola bought at NPR 1,20,000 with NPR 10,000 making charge. Resale rate NPR 1,25,000 with 8% jarti: Net = 1 × 0.92 × 1,25,000 = NPR 1,15,000. Despite a NPR 5,000 gold price rise, the investor lost NPR 15,000 due to jewelry deductions!

10. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Nepal

Common Pitfall

Counting ornamental jewelry with stones as an investment asset at full purchase invoice price.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Value jewelry strictly at 75%-80% of current gold weight value to account for making charges and jarti deductions.

Why: Jewelry making fees are consumer expenses, not recoverable capital.
Common Pitfall

Allocating more than 20% of net worth into gold bullion.

RisePaisa Best Practice

Cap gold at 5% to 10% of liquid net worth; allocate the remaining 90% to productive cash-flow generating assets like SIPs, debentures, and NEPSE equities.

Why: Gold creates zero ongoing income; over-allocating starves your portfolio of compound interest.

12. Knowledge Check & Review Questions

Currently, Nepal does not have an active sovereign digital gold bond market like India. Physical gold coins (Asarfi) from Nepal Rastra Bank Mint Division or reputed jewelers are the primary legal method.

Historically, over 20-30 year horizons, gold has preserved purchasing power against NPR inflation (averaging 7%-9% annual price appreciation), but it has trailed behind productive NEPSE equity compounding and real estate.

Financial advisors recommend capping physical gold bullion at 5% to 10% of total liquid net worth as a crisis insurance buffer.
